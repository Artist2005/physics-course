Zentrale Grundgleichungen in SI-Einheiten

Discipline Physical, Chemical or Biophysical Law Governing Mathematical Equation Key Variables & SI Units
Classical Mechanics Conservation of Momentum & Inelastic Loss m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = m₁u₁ + m₂u₂,   ΔQ = ½μ·Δv²(1 − e²) Mass m (kg), velocity v (m/s), restitution e
Rotational Dynamics Rolling Kinetic Energy & Gyroscopic Precession Ek = ½mv² + ½Iω²,   Ωp = mgl / (Is ωs) Moment of inertia I (kg·m²), spin rate ωs (rad/s)
Fluid Mechanics Bernoulli's Principle, Torricelli's Law & Stokes Drag P + ½ρv² + ρgy = const,   v = √(2gh),   Fd = 6πηrv Static pressure P (Pa), density ρ (kg/m³), viscosity η (Pa·s)
Thermodynamics Ideal Gas Equation, RMS Speed & Carnot Efficiency PV = νRT,   vrms = √(3RT/M),   η = 1 − TC/TH Absolute temperature T (K), molar amount ν (mol)
Wave Optics Snell's Law, Young's Double-Slit & Malus's Law n₁ sin α = n₂ sin γ,   Δy = λL/d,   I = I₀ cos² θ Refractive index n, wavelength λ (nm)
Electromagnetism Lorentz Force, Faraday Induction & RLC Resonance FL = |q|vB sin α,   ℰ = −N dΦ/dt,   ω₀ = 1/√(LC) Magnetic field B (T), inductance L (H), capacitance C (F)
Quantum Physics Photoelectric Equation, Compton Shift & Bohr Levels hc/λ = φ + eUs,   Δλ = λC(1 − cos θ),   En = −13.6/n² eV Planck constant h (J·s), stopping voltage Us (V)
Physical Chemistry Gibbs Free Energy, Arrhenius Kinetics & Buffer pH ΔG = ΔH − TΔS = −RT ln K,   k = A e^{−Ea/RT},   pH = pKa + lg([A⁻]/[HA]) Activation energy Ea (kJ/mol), equilibrium constant K
Electrochemistry Nernst Potential & Faraday's Law of Electrolysis E = E° + (RT/zF) ln([Ox]/[Red]),   m = (M·I·t) / (z·F) Faraday constant F = 96 485 C/mol, current I (A)
Biophysics Nernst Membrane Potential & Hagen-Poiseuille Flow Vm = (RT/zF) ln([C]o/[C]i),   Q = (πΔP·R⁴) / (8ηL) Vessel radius R (mm), volumetric blood flow Q (m³/s)
