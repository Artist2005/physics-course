Frequently asked questions for teachers and students

How can teachers share a 3D lab setup with preset parameters for homework?

Open any experiment on physics.sheua.com, set the desired slider values, and click Share Parameter Link to copy a direct URL that loads those exact initial conditions for students.

Does the virtual lab include a distraction-free projector mode for classrooms?

Yes, clicking Classroom Mode in the top header hides all non-instructional panels and advertisements while enlarging live telemetry readouts for classroom projection.
