Archimedes' Principle & Flotation Conditions

A body immersed in a fluid experiences an upward buoyant force equal to the weight of the fluid displaced by the submerged portion of the body.

Core Curriculum · Grade 7
Mechanism

Hydrostatic pressure in a stationary fluid of density ρ_fluid increases linearly with depth: p(h) = p₀ + ρ_fluid g h. Because the upward pressure on the bottom surface of a submerged body exceeds the downward pressure on its top, integrating pressure forces over the body’s surface yields an upward Archimedean buoyant force F_A.

Equations and conservation laws

By Archimedes’ principle, the buoyant force equals the weight of the displaced fluid: F_A = ρ_fluid g V_sub. Vertical motion of a body of total volume V and density ρ_body is governed by the interplay between weight mg = ρ_body g V, buoyancy F_A, and fluid drag.

Measurements and applications

Three regimes emerge from the density ratio: 1) if ρ_body ρ_fluid, it sinks to the bottom with apparent weight Wₐₚₚ = (ρ_body - ρ_fluid)gV.

Experimental parameters and SI units
  • Body density ρ_body: 200 … 1800 kg/m³, standard value 600 kg/m³
  • Fluid density ρ_fluid: 700 … 2000 kg/m³, standard value 1000 kg/m³
  • Cube side length a: 0.8 … 1.6 m, standard value 1 m
  • Fluid viscosity η: 0.1 … 1.2 Pa·s, standard value 0.35 Pa·s
Physical model and application
The flotation of steel vessels is governed by the ratio of average hull density to fluid density: the hydrostatic pressure difference between the bottom and top surfaces of a submerged body generates an upward Archimedes buoyant force equal to the weight of displaced fluid.
F_A = ρ_fluid g V_sub, F_g = ρ_body g V₀ (V_sub)/(V₀) = (ρ_body)/(ρ_fluid) (for ρ_body ≤ ρ_fluid)
Question
A solid cube of density ρ_body = 600 kg/m³ is placed into fresh water (ρ_water = 1000 kg/m³). What fraction of the cube's volume will be submerged in equilibrium?
Selected answer Option B
In static equilibrium of a floating body, gravity is balanced by the Archimedes buoyant force: ρ_body g V₀ = ρ_fluid g V_sub. Hence the submerged fraction is V_sub/V₀ = ρ_body/ρ_fluid = 600/1000 = 0.6 (60%). If ρ_body > ρ_fluid, the body sinks.
