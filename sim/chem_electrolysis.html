Electrolytic Cell & Faraday’s Laws of Electrolysis

The mass of substance liberated at an electrode during electrolysis is directly proportional to the total electric charge Q = I·t and the molar equivalent mass M/z.

Question
According to Faraday's laws m = (M · I · t)/(z · F), how does the molar amount of metal plated on the cathode for a given charge Q = It change when switching from a monovalent ion Ag+ (z = 1) to a divalent ion Cu2+ (z = 2)?
Selected answer Option B
By Faraday's combined law of electrolysis, passing 1 F = 96 485 C of charge deposits exactly 1 mol of equivalents (ν = (I t)/(z F)). For aqueous CuSO4 (z = 2), metallic copper plates onto the cathode while water oxidizes at the inert anode to evolve oxygen gas O2↑ (z = 4).

Core Curriculum · Grade 9-11
Mechanism

Electrolysis is a non-spontaneous redox process (Δ G > 0) driven by an external DC power supply across electrodes immersed in an electrolyte. Unlike a galvanic cell, in an electrolytic cell the cathode is the negative electrode (supplying electrons for reduction of cations), while the anode is the positive electrode (withdrawing electrons for oxidation).

Equations and conservation laws

During electrolysis of aqueous CuSO4 with inert graphite or platinum electrodes, Cu2+ ions are preferentially reduced at the cathode (E° = +0.34 V): Cu2+ + 2e- → Cu0↓. At the inert anode, oxygenated SO42- anions resist oxidation, so water molecules are oxidized instead, evolving oxygen gas bubbles: 2H2O - 4e- → O2↑ + 4H+.

Measurements and applications

Quantitative electrode yields obey Faraday's combined law of electrolysis: m = (M· I· t)/(z· F), where Q = I· t is total passed charge, z is the electron stoichiometry per ion, and F = e NA ≈ 96 485 C/mol is Faraday's constant (the charge of one mole of electrons).

Experimental parameters and SI units
  • Direct electrolysis current I: 1 … 10 A, standard value 5 A
  • Electrolysis duration t: 5 … 30 min, standard value 15 min
  • Cation charge number z (1=Ag⁺, 2=Cu²⁺, 3=Au³⁺): 1 … 3, standard value 2
  • DC power supply voltage U: 2 … 8 V, standard value 4.5 V
Physical model and application
While a battery generates current from a spontaneous reaction, electrolysis uses an external power supply to drive a reaction in reverse, plating dissolved blue Cu2+ ions as shiny metallic copper onto the cathode. This is how jewelry is gold-plated and industrial copper is refined.
