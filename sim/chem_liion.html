CHAPTER XXXVII · § C-37. LITHIUM-ION BATTERY & ROCKING-CHAIR INTERCALATION t = 0.00 s

Lithium-Ion Battery (Rocking-Chair Intercalation): LiC₆ + CoO₂ ⇄ C₆ + LiCoO₂

"By shuttling lithium ions reversibly between two layered host lattices, we created a rechargeable battery that transformed portable electronics.", M. Stanley Whittingham, John Goodenough & Akira Yoshino, Nobel Prize 2019

Question
How does a rechargeable lithium-ion battery store and release electrical energy without forming metallic lithium dendrites during normal operation?
Selected answer Option B
A Li-ion cell operates via topotactic intercalation of Li+ ions between two layered host lattices separated by a porous polymer separator in a non-aqueous electrolyte (LiPF6 in EC/DMC): **Anode (-)**: graphene sheets of graphite LixC6 (E° ≈ -3.0 V); **Cathode (+)**: layered cobalt oxide Li1-xCoO2 (E° ≈ +0.7 V). During discharge (mode=1), Li+ ions migrate from graphite through the separator into CoO2 while electrons power the external load at U ≈ 3.6-4.2 V. During charging (mode=2), an external charger drives Li+ back into the graphite layers.

Mechanism

A rechargeable Lithium-Ion Battery stores energy via reversible intercalation ("rocking-chair" shuttle) of Li+ ions between two layered host crystals separated by a microporous separator in a non-aqueous organic electrolyte.

Equations and conservation laws

The negative electrode consists of graphene sheets in **graphite (LixC6)** (E° ≈ -3.0 V), while the positive electrode is a layered transition-metal oxide such as **Li1-xCoO2** (E° ≈ +0.7 V), delivering a cell voltage of **3.6-4.2 V**.

Measurements and applications

During discharge, Li+ ions de-intercalate from the graphite anode (LixC6 → C6 + xLi+ + xe-) and intercalate into the CoO2 cathode while electrons power the external load; during charging, an external voltage reverses both ion and electron flows.

Experimental parameters and SI units
  • Operating mode (1 = Discharge to load, 2 = Charge): 1 … 2, standard value 1
  • State of Charge SoC (x in LixC₆): 10 … 95 %, standard value 75 %
  • C-rate current I: 0.5 … 4 C, standard value 1.5 C
  • Cell temperature t: 0 … 50 °C, standard value 25 °C
Physical model and application
A lithium-ion battery operates by reversible intercalation of Li+ cations between two layered host lattices (graphite C6 and LiCoO2). Charging intercalates Li+ ions between the graphene sheets of the anode, while discharging shuttles them across the separator into the CoO2 cathode as electrons deliver electrical work at 3.7 V.
