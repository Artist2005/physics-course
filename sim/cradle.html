Newton's Cradle

Simultaneous satisfaction of momentum and mechanical energy conservation uniquely dictates not only the total momentum, but also the exact number of rebounding spheres.

Question
In a Newton's cradle of 5 identical steel spheres, N = 2 spheres on the left are pulled back and released together with impact speed v. Why cannot just 1 sphere fly out on the right with double speed 2v?
Selected answer Option B
If a single sphere rebounded at speed 2v, its momentum would be 2mv (momentum conserved), but its kinetic energy would be (1)/(2)m(2v)2 = 2mv2, whereas the initial kinetic energy of the two incoming spheres was 2·(1)/(2)mv2 = mv2. The unique solution to both conservation equations is that Nout = Nin spheres rebound with speed u = v!

Core Curriculum · Grade 9
Mechanism

Newton's cradle shows that conservation of momentum alone is insufficient to uniquely determine the outcome of a collision, both linear momentum and kinetic energy must be conserved simultaneously. When N identical spheres of mass m strike a stationary row at velocity v0, a rapid elastic compression pulse (Hertzian contact wave) propagates through the chain.

Equations and conservation laws

Suppose k spheres were ejected at velocity u after N spheres struck at speed v0. Enforcing momentum conservation Nmv0 = kmu and kinetic energy conservation N(mv02)/(2) = k(mu2)/(2) and dividing the two equations immediately yields u = v0 and therefore **k = N**. Exactly the same number of spheres must swing out on the far side!

Measurements and applications

When the restitution coefficient e < 1 (accounting for internal acoustic losses and imperfect steel elasticity), each impact dissipates a small fraction of mechanical energy into heat Q, gradually exciting small collective oscillations across all spheres.

Experimental parameters and SI units
  • Lifted spheres N: 1 … 3 pcs, standard value 2 pcs
  • Release angle θ₀: 15 … 55 °, standard value 40 °
  • Steel restitution e: 0.7 … 1, standard value 0.99
  • Suspension length L: 2.4 … 4.2 m, standard value 3.4 m
Physical model and application
In Newton's cradle, releasing one outer steel sphere causes exactly one sphere on the opposite end to swing out at the same speed, because an elastic collision simultaneously conserves both total linear momentum and total kinetic energy.
