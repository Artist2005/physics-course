Single-Slit Wavefront Diffraction in 3D

When a slit narrows toward the wavelength of light, the beam does not shrink, it fans out into a broad diffraction pattern.

Question
A plane monochromatic light wavefront passes through a narrow vertical slit of width b. What happens to the width of the central bright maximum Δ ycentral on the viewing screen if the slit width b is decreased?
Selected answer Option B
By the Huygens-Fresnel principle, secondary coherent wavelets across the slit interfere destructively at angles where b sin θ = ± mλ. The half-width of the central maximum is set by the first minimum (m=1): y1 ≈ (λ L)/(b). Narrowing the slit b increases diffraction spreading and broadens the central peak Δ y = (2λ L)/(b)!

Core Curriculum · Grade 11
Mechanism

Diffraction is the bending and spreading of a wave as it passes through an aperture or around an obstacle, violating geometric ray optics whenever the aperture width a is comparable to the wavelength λ. By the Huygens-Fresnel principle, every infinitesimal strip across the open slit of width a radiates coherent secondary wavelets.

Equations and conservation laws

In the far-field (Fraunhofer diffraction) at angle θ, the path difference between wavelets from the two slit edges is Δ = a sin θ. Dividing the slit into 2m equal zones shows that destructive cancellation occurs at **diffraction minima a sin θ = ± mλ** (m = 1, 2, 3, …). Integrating across the continuous aperture gives the intensity profile I(θ) = I0 (( sin β)/(β))2, where β = (π a sin θ)/(λ).

Measurements and applications

Over 84% of the diffracted light power is concentrated in the central bright maximum bounded by sin θ1 = ± (λ)/(a), with screen width W = (2λ L)/(a). This reveals a fundamental wave reciprocity: **narrowing the slit a causes the diffracted beam on the screen to spread wider!**

Experimental parameters and SI units
  • Diffraction slit width b: 1.2 … 4.5 μm, standard value 2.2 μm
  • Light wavelength λ: 400 … 700 nm, standard value 550 nm
  • Screen distance L: 3 … 6.5 m, standard value 4.5 m
  • Diffraction minimum order m: 1 … 3, standard value 1
Physical model and application
Waves bend around obstacles and spread into the geometric shadow region through diffraction. When a monochromatic laser beam passes through a narrow slit, it forms a broadened central maximum flanked by side fringes: narrowing the slit width a causes the diffraction pattern to spread wider.
