Dispersion of Light & Spectrum Formation in a Prism

A narrow beam of white light in a glass prism splits into a rainbow spectrum because short violet waves have a lower phase velocity in glass than long red waves.

Prediction Problem
A white light beam passes through a triangular glass prism. Rays of which color are refracted through the largest deviation angle \delta and why?
Your hypothesis: Option B
Under normal dispersion, the refractive index increases monotonically as wavelength decreases according to Cauchy's law n(\lambda) = n_0 + B/\lambda^2. Since \lambda_{\text{viol}} n_{\text{red}} and violet rays bend most toward the prism base.
