Projectile Launched at an Angle to the Horizontal

Ballistic motion in a uniform gravitational field is a superposition of uniform horizontal motion along Ox and uniformly accelerated vertical motion along Oy.

Question
A projectile is launched at θ = 45° to the horizontal in vacuum (cd = 0). What is its kinetic energy Ek at the highest point (apex) of the trajectory compared to its initial kinetic energy Ek0 = (1)/(2)mv02?
Selected answer Option B
At the vertex of the parabola only the vertical velocity component vanishes (vy = 0), while the horizontal component vx = v0 cos θ remains constant. Therefore, the minimum kinetic energy at the apex is Ek,min = (1)/(2)m(v0 cos θ)2 = Ek0 cos 2θ.

Core Curriculum · Grade 9-10
Mechanism

Projectile motion launched at angle α with initial speed v0 illustrates Galileo's principle of independence of motions. In vacuum (k = 0), no horizontal force acts along Ox, keeping vx = v0 cos α = const, while along the vertical axis Oy gravity produces constant downward acceleration ay = -g.

Equations and conservation laws

Eliminating time t = (x)/(v0 cos α) from x(t) = v0 cos α · t and y(t) = v0 sin α · t - (1)/(2)gt2 yields the parabolic trajectory equation: y(x) = x tan α - (gx2)/(2v02 cos 2α). The time of flight is tflight = (2v0 sin α)/(g), the apex height is H = (v02 sin 2α)/(2g), and the horizontal range L = (v02 sin (2α))/(g) peaks at α = 45°.

Measurements and applications

When linear aerodynamic drag (Fd = -kv) is introduced, horizontal velocity decays exponentially as vx(t) = v0xe-(k/m)t, the descending branch becomes steeper than the ascending branch (Tartaglia's ballistic curve), and the optimal launch angle for maximum range shifts below 45°.

Experimental parameters and SI units
  • Initial velocity v₀: 6 … 15 m/s, standard value 11.5 m/s
  • Launch angle θ: 15 … 80 °, standard value 45 °
  • Initial height h₀: 0.2 … 4.5 m, standard value 1.2 m
  • Air drag coeff. cd: 0 … 0.15 kg/m, standard value 0 kg/m
  • Projectile mass m: 0.5 … 5 kg, standard value 1.5 kg
Physical model and application
For a projectile launched at an angle to the horizontal, maximum range in vacuum is achieved at a launch angle of 45°. In a real atmosphere, aerodynamic drag opposes velocity v, dissipating mechanical energy and shortening the horizontal range.
